3 · Add it in Gmail

  1. Open Gmail on a computer → ⚙ Settings → See all settings.
  2. On the General tab, scroll to Signature → + Create new → name it “Sory & Partners”.
  3. Click in the signature box and paste (Ctrl+V on Windows, ⌘+V on Mac).
  4. Under Signature defaults, choose it for For new emails use and On reply/forward use.
  5. Scroll to the bottom and click Save Changes. Send yourself a test email to check.
